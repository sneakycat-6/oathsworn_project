About

Into the Deepwood

Oathsworn is a fully co-operative dungeon-crawl board game driven by a rich narrative and brutal, deeply tactical combat.

This companion app is designed to support your sessions — tracking what matters, surfacing the right information at the right moment, so you can focus on the story unfolding across the table.

Built by the community, for the community. Open-source, no accounts, no ads — your campaign data stays yours.

🗡

Session-Ready

Everything loads locally, no internet required once the page is open.

📜

Your Data, Saved

Progress is persisted in your browser's local storage — pick up exactly where you left off.

🌑

Dark Fantasy Theme

Crafted to match the grim, atmospheric world of Oathsworn.

⚙

Extensible

Modular codebase — add new pages and tools without touching what already works.
